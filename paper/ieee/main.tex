\begin{abstract}
Modern data tables let users define columns in natural language: each cell
is computed by a language model from a versioned prompt over its row. When
the prompt is edited, systems face a choice: recompute every cell,
expensive and rate-limited, or silently reuse stale ones, with unbounded
error. We present semantic incremental view maintenance (\sivm{}), a
layer that certifies which cached cells may be reused after a prompt
edit, bounding the false-reuse rate of each certified stratum by a
user-chosen error budget with stated confidence. \sivm{} freezes
cached-value strata, samples adaptively on a doubling look schedule, and
bounds each stratum's flip rate with an exact finite-population
confidence bound; a strict mode certifies at a deflated threshold to
bound the error of the reused cells alone. An impossibility floor
follows for this estimand: a model's rate of disagreement with itself
under the unedited prompt limits the certifiable budget. On two public corpora (\corpusSO\
Stack Overflow developer profiles; \corpusDjinni\ Djinni candidate
profiles) with \labeledCells\ labeled cells, a formatting-only edit certifies \benchSavingsMain\ call savings at an error budget of 0.2 with
\benchRealized\ realized false-reuse, and at deployment scale
\deployOracle\ oracle calls certify reuse of \deployReused\ of \deployN\
cells. Across \calTrials\ calibration runs with planted flip rates,
unsafe certificates occur in at most \calUnsafeWorst\ of runs in any
configuration, against a nominal per-stratum budget of \calNominalDelta.
A second certification \driftDaysLater\ days later, against the same
cache, measured provider-side model drift as a higher flip rate and
re-certified the same budgets, the tighter one a look deeper than on
the August draws. Certification reproduces on
\famCertCount\ of \famCount\ further model families, while which edits
are benign varies by family. We release the system under a source-available
noncommercial license, with the labels and a benchmark of versioned
prompt-edit pairs.
\end{abstract}
