\textbf{Cover letter for the resubmission of Access-2026-41149, ``Reuse, but Verify: Certified Maintenance of Table Cells Computed by Large Language Models under Prompt Edits''}

Dear Editors,

I am resubmitting the manuscript above, which was reviewed under manuscript ID Access-2026-41149 and returned on 29 September 2026 with a decision of reject with resubmission allowed. I am grateful to the three reviewers: their comments were specific, and acting on them made the paper better rather than merely longer. The point-by-point response is uploaded under ``Author's Response Files''; the highlighted PDF marks every change.

\textbf{What changed.} The reviewers' central concern was that the headline results rested on a concentration bound (Maurer--Pontil) that the paper's own theorem did not prove for the sampling the procedure performs. The revision replaces it with the exact finite-population (hypergeometric) bound, which is valid for exactly that sampling by a short argument and is tighter than the empirical-Bernstein alternatives on a binary loss; Theorem 1 is now fully proven for the bound used, and every number in the manuscript was regenerated from the same stored labels and oracle draws. The results improved on every axis the reviewers raised: more configurations certify, at higher savings, on fewer oracle calls, and the deployment certificate a reviewer re-derived clears both budgets. Two studies were added: a direct test of the cross-row independence assumption, and a live re-certification of the deployment column 55 days after the original run, which measured provider-side model drift and showed the certifier refusing the budget the drift had made unsafe. The clarity requests (terminology before use, a notation table, an algorithm box, an appendix for the checker conditions, a reconciliation table for the flip-rate figures, model identifiers, product citations, reference locators, the template footer) are all addressed.

\textbf{Prior dissemination (please read before the similarity report).} An earlier version of this manuscript, under the title ``Reuse, but Verify: Certified Maintenance of LLM-Computed Table Cells under Prompt Edits,'' is posted as a preprint on Zenodo, doi:10.5281/zenodo.21833641, deposited by me on 7 August 2026. It is a preprint only: it has not been submitted to, reviewed by, or published in any journal or conference. The CrossCheck/iThenticate report will show high similarity to that single Zenodo record and to my own first submission of this manuscript; both are my own earlier drafts, not duplicate publication. On acceptance I will update the Zenodo record to carry the IEEE citation and DOI.
