\textbf{Cover letter for the submission ``Reuse, but Verify: Certified Maintenance of Table Cells Computed by Large Language Models under Prompt Edits''}

Dear Editor,

I am submitting the manuscript ``Reuse, but Verify: Certified Maintenance of Table Cells Computed by Large Language Models under Prompt Edits'' for consideration as a Research Article in IEEE Access.

\textbf{Contribution.} Tables whose columns are computed by a language model from a natural-language prompt are now common in data-enrichment and analytics tools. When the prompt is edited, today's systems either recompute every cell or silently reuse stale ones. The paper formalizes the maintenance question, gives a certification procedure (sIVM) that bounds the expected false-reuse rate of each reused stratum by a user budget with a stated confidence, proves an impossibility floor set by the model's own self-disagreement rate, and evaluates on two public corpora with over 32,000 labeled cells, 1,000-replication bootstraps, and 396,000 known-rate calibration trials, with a replication across four further model families. All code, labels, and result artifacts are public, and every number in the manuscript is generated from those artifacts.
