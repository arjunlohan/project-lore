\textbf{Cover letter for the resubmission of Access-2026-41149, ``Reuse, but Verify: Certified Maintenance of Table Cells Computed by Large Language Models under Prompt Edits''}

Dear Editors,

I am resubmitting the manuscript above, which was reviewed under manuscript ID Access-2026-41149 and returned on 29 September 2026 with a decision of reject with resubmission allowed. I am grateful to the three reviewers; acting on their comments made the paper better. The point-by-point response is uploaded under ``Author's Response Files''. The highlighted PDF marks added and changed text with a yellow highlight (the text itself is set in blue, changed numbers included) and omits deleted text. Table bodies, the figures, and the algorithm are compared as whole blocks and marked where new or regenerated, as all of them are; the reference list keeps the clean copy's numbering, with new or changed entries marked.

\textbf{What changed.} The reviewers' central concern was that the headline results rested on a concentration bound (Maurer--Pontil) that the paper's own theorem did not prove for the sampling the procedure performs. The revision replaces it with the exact finite-population (hypergeometric) bound, which is valid for exactly that sampling and tighter on a binary loss; Theorem {{ref:thm:valid}} is now proven in full for the bound used, and every number was regenerated from the same stored labels and oracle draws. The certification results improved: more configurations certify ({{m:boundExactConfigs}} of {{m:boundConfigsTotal}} on the main seed instead of {{m:boundEbConfigs}}), at higher savings, on fewer oracle calls, and the deployment certificate a reviewer re-derived clears both budgets. Five studies were added: the remaining edit pairs on further models and a sixth model, so that five further models carry all five pairs, each replicated over {{m:bootB}} sampling seeds; a direct test of the cross-row independence assumption (the flip rates of completed requests agree; dependence among concurrent draws is not excluded, and its cost is simulated); a free-text pilot with a judge-based equivalence relation calibrated on hand labels (a negative result on power, reported as one); a re-scoring of the select column's formatting edit under a graded relation; and a live re-certification of the deployment column {{m:driftDaysLater}} days after the original run, which measured drift of the served model and certified both budgets again. For Reviewer 2, Section {{ref:sec:problem}} now opens with terminology and a table of terms and notation, states Assumptions 1 to 3 together, and gives the correctness criterion in words; Algorithm {{ref:alg:sivm}} states the procedure; and a positioning table (Table {{ref:tab:related}}) sets sIVM against each neighboring line. For Reviewer 3, sentences were shortened throughout (from {{stat:v1.mean}} to {{stat:now.mean}} words on average), Section {{ref:sec:experiments}} was divided into subsections with the main results directly after the setup, and number-dense detail moved to tables and appendices, so that the main text carries fewer numbers than the first submission did ({{stat:main.numbers}} against {{stat:v1.numbers}}). The remaining requests are all addressed in the response.
