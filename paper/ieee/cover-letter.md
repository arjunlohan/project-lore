# Cover letter (paste into the IEEE Author Portal "Comments to the Editor" / cover-letter field)

Dear Editor,

I am submitting the manuscript "Reuse, but Verify: Certified Maintenance of Table Cells Computed by Large Language Models under Prompt Edits" for consideration as a Research Article in IEEE Access.

**Contribution.** Tables whose columns are computed by a language model from a natural-language prompt are now common in data-enrichment and analytics tools. When the prompt is edited, today's systems either recompute every cell or silently reuse stale ones. The paper formalizes the maintenance question, gives a certification procedure (sIVM) that bounds the expected false-reuse rate of each reused stratum by a user budget with a stated confidence, proves an impossibility floor set by the model's own self-disagreement rate, and evaluates on two public corpora with over 32,000 labeled cells, 1,000-replication bootstraps, and 396,000 known-rate calibration trials. All code, labels, and result artifacts are public, and every number in the manuscript is generated from those artifacts.

**Prior dissemination (please read before the similarity report).** An earlier version of this manuscript, under the title "Reuse, but Verify: Certified Maintenance of LLM-Computed Table Cells under Prompt Edits," is posted as a preprint on Zenodo, doi:10.5281/zenodo.21833641, deposited by me on 7 August 2026. It is a preprint only: it has not been submitted to, reviewed by, or published in any journal or conference. Because it is a near-verbatim earlier draft of this same manuscript, the CrossCheck/iThenticate report will show very high similarity to that single Zenodo record; that overlap is my own preprint, not duplicate publication. In line with IEEE's preprint policy, on acceptance I will update the Zenodo record to carry the IEEE citation and DOI.
