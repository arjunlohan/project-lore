## Consensus verdict

The expensive thing was done right. `exp11-fullscale.json` is a genuine live full-corpus run: 89,184 rows materialized through the production runner, 7 cell errors, 89,177 covered, 25,801,689 ms of materialization (7.17 h), then live-oracle certification at two budgets with the real model, 135 oracle calls, 81,373 cells reused, savings 0.9124886, certification wall-clock 192,808 ms (193 s). That closes F9, the item reviewer C called the single highest-impact addition available, and it retroactively earns most of Contribution 3's "not a simulation harness" claim, which had been resting on a 179-second replay over frozen labels since round 1. Four more r3 items closed at their core: the calibration attribution is now correct per metric and states the strictly stronger true claim (violationRatePresented = 0 in all 96 exp9 configurations, zero certifications at p > alpha in all 18 unsafe ones, zero strict-mode exceedances anywhere); the tau sweep is a real measured seven-point per-row experiment on disk; 2.76% and 63.7% now match their artifacts; and the abstract and Contribution 2 finally describe the pinned value-only mechanism rather than the losing ablation.

The statistical spine survives direct audit at a level the panel has not previously reached. Three reviewers independently re-derived all 20 `table1.tex` rows and every `figdata.tex` coordinate from exp8/exp9 and got digit-identical output. Both deployment empirical-Bernstein bounds reproduce to six digits from `sivm.ts` (0.191585 at n=90/k=1, and 0.073283 at n=720/k=24 with delta_look = 0.05/6). The exp11 arithmetic reproduces exactly across the board: savings 0.9124886 to 91.2%, certifyWallMs 192,808 to 193 s, materializationMs 25,801,689 to 7.17 h, covered 89,177 against n 89,184 to 7 errors. The exp10 label statistics reproduce from the raw 12,000 draws (diagonals 7.157/7.100/7.404%, mean 7.2203%, vote-3 126/2,000 = 6.30%, self-flip 302/5,996 = 5.04% and 296/5,998 = 4.93%). Nobody on this panel disputes the mathematics any more.

What blocks acceptance is that the revision reintroduced the disease in the abstract and in its own flagship paragraph. The exp7 rerun that r3 mandated happened at 19:14 on Aug 4 and `main.tex` was edited at 02:37 the next morning, and not one of the three paragraphs the rerun invalidated was regenerated: the paper still says aggregate-only certification "certifies 64% savings with aggregate error 6.2%" at alpha=0.1 where the current artifact records `certified: false, reused: 0, savings: 0` on both edits, and the abstract's headline finding (iv) still prints 47.6% subgroup error where the artifact now says 44.1%. The Setup paragraph asserts total model spend "under $4" seventy-eight lines above a paragraph reporting a $9.31 experiment, in a paper whose entire contribution is cost, and neither $9.31 nor "78,956 fresh computations" reproduces from any artifact (the ledger records $9.38559188, and the paper's own $0.82 derivation only works from 9.3856). And F5, an unscoped "realized error never exceeded its bound" sentence that r3 graded reject-level and checkable in seconds, still sits one paragraph above the 2.1% exceed cell that refutes it.

The panel converges on the diagnosis and it is structural, not editorial. The r3 close-out path listed one item first: make `gen-paper-assets.ts` emit a macro file for every abstract, prose, and caption number, then grep every literal. That step was skipped; the generator still writes only `table1.tex` and `figdata.tex`. Every new defect in this revision is downstream of skipping it. The mechanically generated parts of this paper are impeccable and have been for two rounds. The hand-typed parts have now been wrong in three consecutive rounds, and this round they are wrong in the abstract. Sixteen round-3 items are additionally untouched, including the entire bibliography (Evergreen still arXiv:2603.08907, BARGAIN's invented title, Stale View Cleaning still absent after three rounds of "cite or die", `\begin{thebibliography}{19}` over 15 entries) and the entire PVLDB apparatus (article class, "co-authors TBD", no availability statement, and zero `\ref{fig` anywhere in the source, so neither figure is cited from the body).

The remaining work is again almost entirely zero-API: one macro pass plus a literal sweep, one prose regeneration from files already on disk, one recomputation of the deployment overlap from cells already stored, the exp9 grid extension (pure simulation), and the apparatus, which is writing rather than computing. Two cheap spends would change the paper's standing: a certification-cost distribution (zero API, simulate the pinned schedule at the measured stratum rate) and a second model family on the SO formatting pair, which costs roughly 5% of what exp11 already spent. Under the standard the panel set in round 2 and reaffirmed in round 3 (every mandatory item closed, zero text-artifact contradictions), a referee running the panel's own checks still falsifies the paper against its own repository inside two minutes, one of the falsifications in the abstract and one about money.

### R5. [reject-level (E), major (A,C,D) · MANDATORY · 5/5] The deployment run's realized-error check is not reproducible and is unfiltered by cell status

## Panel disagreements, resolved

Three points where reviewers diverged, adjudicated against the artifacts.

1. **Is the deployment verification a biased subsample?** No, not on the row-selection axis. A verified that the ground-truth pool comes from a seeded uniform SQL sample fixed before and independently of the certification, and that `gt2k` is snapshotted before the sweeps, so conditional on the certified stratum the rows are a uniform without-replacement subsample and 52/2,047 is unbiased for the stratum flip rate. 2. **Severity of the B3 and tau-paragraph mismatch (R4).** Graded reject-level by B, major by C/D/E, minor by A.
