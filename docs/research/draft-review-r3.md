## Consensus verdict

The hard, load-bearing round-2 cluster genuinely landed, and the panel did not take it on faith. The PRNG repair is real: `packages/core/src/sivm.ts` implements canonical mulberry32 (Math.imul, exact 32-bit semantics) with a correct Fisher-Yates `seededShuffle`, both replication machines were rerun on it, and `paper/table1.tex` plus `paper/figdata.tex` are digit-identical to regeneration from the current JSONs; multiple reviewers independently reproduced all 20 table rows and every figure coordinate. The N1 self-refutation is fixed the strong way: the abstract is estimand-scoped and true against the artifacts (violationRatePresented = 0 in all 40 exp8 bootstrap configurations and all 96 exp9 configurations; strict reuse-set mode zero exceedances everywhere; zero certifications at p > alpha in 192,000 trials), and the featured 19%/2.1% frontier cell (2 of 94 certifying replications) is printed and narrated as the adverse-selection signature rather than denied. The 5.0% noise floor is honestly adopted and reproduces from the raw 12,000-draw labels. The fabricated SUPG stub is deleted, the oracle-cost column is restored, and the deflated-threshold reuse-set mode with its census guard is implemented exactly as theorized.

What blocks acceptance is that the revision then re-commits the same disease class it was sent away to cure, in new places. Five text-vs-artifact contradictions are live: (1) the calibration paragraph attributes exp9's exceedances to the presented estimand when the artifact records presented violations as zero in all 96 configurations (the truthful statement is strictly stronger than what the paper confesses); (2) prose says realized presented-error 2.26% where the regenerated table two paragraphs away says 2.76%; (3) the caption's ablation figure 63.5% matches no artifact in any round (current: 63.7%); (4) the Limitations sampling range "11-45% of cells" is derivable from no reading of exp8 (true certifying range roughly 7-81%); (5) an unscoped "realized error never exceeded its bound" sentence sits one paragraph from the 2.1% cell that refutes it. On top of that, two paragraphs still describe things that never happened (the tau sweep, N7; the 90-vs-348 futility economics, N8), the abstract and Contribution 2 still sell the losing ablation's mechanism against the pinned artifact's own value-only procedure string (N5), the proof sketch asserts the exact WoR conservatism that A3 now disclaims, the impossibility floor is still stated at a granularity the paper's own strata refute, and the r2 items that cost writing effort or API dollars (N4, S2, S3, S6, S7) were left essentially untouched.

The distance is short and the panel agrees on its shape: almost everything remaining is zero-API prose, citation, regeneration, and replay work (labels are frozen, the calibration is pure simulation, the exp10 draws are on disk), plus one priced full-corpus certification run (under $25) and naming the model. C flags the payoff the draft is leaving invisible: certification sample sizes are table-size-independent, so the ~900 non-reused cells that cap savings at 66.8% on n=2,000 become roughly 99% certified savings at 89K rows, converting the headline from respectable to startling while discharging Contribution 3's "not a simulation harness" overclaim. Total remaining budget: one honest week and under $50. But under the round-2 panel's own standard (every mandatory item closed, no text-artifact contradictions), a referee running the panel's checks would still falsify the paper against its own artifacts in minute one, so it cannot pass as submitted.

### F1. [reject-level (A,E), major (B,C,D) · MANDATORY · NEW · 5/5] The calibration paragraph misattributes exp9's exceedances to the presented estimand

`paper/main.tex` ~488-508 claims realized presented-estimand error among certified SAFE strata "can exceed alpha (worst 11% of certified runs in a configuration certified 1% of the time)". The artifact says the opposite: violationRatePresented = 0 in all 96 configurations. The 11.1% is violationRateReuseSet (1/9) under presented-mode certificates at (alpha=0.05, n=1800, p=0.04), whose certification rate is 0.45% (9/2,000), not 1%; a second exceedance cell (4/75 = 5.33% at alpha=0.1, n=1800, p=0.08, certification rate 3.75%) is unmentioned. The paper confesses a violation of its certified estimand that never occurred, undersells its strongest calibration result (zero presented-estimand exceedances in 192,000 trials), and reproduces the N1 defect class, estimand mislabeling, in the one paragraph whose job is estimand precision. The sentence was evidently written against the retired LCG-era artifact. Additionally, the paragraph's enumerated grid (8 rates x 3 sizes x 2 estimands x 2,000 trials) multiplies to 96,000; the alpha grid {0.05, 0.1} is still unstated, so the printed 192,000 is not derivable from the text. **Fix:** rewrite from the current artifact: realized presented-estimand error never exceeded alpha in any certified trial of any configuration (a stronger claim); the stricter, uncertified reuse-set criterion under presented-mode certificates exceeded alpha in 1/9 certifying trials of one rare configuration (0.45%) and 4/75 of another (3.75%), the same adverse-selection signature as Table 1's 2.1% cell; strict reuse-set mode had zero exceedances; state the alpha grid so 192,000 reconciles.
