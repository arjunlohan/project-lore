Reviewer letters continue the r1-r4 panel keys by focus area.

**Counts:** 7 of 30 round-4 mandatory items confirmed closed at their core, of which **3 are fully discharged** (F5, R1, R13(b)) · 4 more partially closed (R2, R5, F13, F14) · 14 round-3 items and 10 round-4 items still carry open mandatory components · **13 new findings**, all mandatory (5 reject-level, 5 major, 3 minor) · **37 open mandatory items** in total, up from 30 · **at least 22 live text-versus-artifact contradictions, 14 of them introduced by this revision**, against 7 in round 4.

## Consensus verdict

The r3 and r4 close-out path's item (1) was finally executed and it works. `scripts/experiments/gen-paper-assets.ts` now emits `paper/macros.tex` (86 `\newcommand`s) alongside `table1.tex` and `figdata.tex`, and `main.tex` `\input{macros}` and cites macros for the great majority of prose numbers. Reviewer E re-ran the generator against the live repository and MySQL instance and all three files regenerate byte-identically (`diff` clean, `ASSETS_OK: 20 table rows, 86 macros`); reviewers A, B, and D each independently re-derived between forty and sixty macro values from exp0, exp0b, exp1, exp2, exp4, exp6, exp7, exp8, exp8-embed-ablation, exp9, exp10, exp11 and the live `cost_ledger` and found every mechanically derived one correct. The statistical spine is no longer in dispute by anyone on this panel. All three exp11 empirical-Bernstein bounds reproduce from `sivm.ts` to full floating-point precision (n=90/k=1 gives 0.19158474122511074 against the artifact's ...77; n=360/k=9 gives 0.08790231936350867; n=45/k=16 gives 0.8851145221844834); `n* = 257` reproduces from the stated formula; all 20 `table1.tex` rows and both `figdata.tex` series regenerate digit-identically from exp8/exp9; exp9 records `violationRatePresented = 0` in all 96 configurations and zero certifications in all 18 unsafe ones; the deflated threshold `alpha (n-m)/n` matches `sivm.ts:345-349` exactly and the census guard is present. R1 is fully closed and 47.6% is gone from all three sites, R4(b)(c) are closed, F5 is closed, R13(b) and R14 are closed, the author block and availability statement exist, both figures are now `\ref`'d from the body, and `\begin{thebibliography}{15}` finally matches its 15 entries.

The generator did not end the disease. It moved it upstream, where `grep main.tex` no longer finds it. Eight macros are hand-typed literals inside the generator (`floorBoolFresh`, `floorBoolPairs`, `deployErrors`, `deployMatHours`, `wideningTrueFlip`, `corpusSO`, `corpusDjinni`, `labeledCells`, plus `deployMaintMinutes` computed from the hardcoded 7.2), of which two are already provably wrong against their own artifacts. Twelve of the 86 macros are defined and never used, and they are exactly the values the prose still types by hand: `aucVcacheF`, `aucVcacheW`, `aucInterF`, `aucInterW`, `simLo`, `simHi`, `benchCertRate`, `frontierD`, `calConfigs`, `djFormattingFlip`, `djCriteriaFlip`, `expTwoFormattingFlip`. Two of those unused macros disagree with the prose written for the same sentence: the macro says AUC 0.468 and `main.tex:373` prints 0.467; `\simLo`/`\simHi` are 0.96/0.99 and the prose prints "the score's entire range is 0.94--0.99", which silently unions two edits. Roughly twenty further hand-typed numerals survive in `main.tex`. And the availability statement asserts "no value here is transcribed by hand", a claim a referee falsifies in under a minute and the worst possible sentence to be wrong about in a paper on this trajectory.

Three defects are worse than bookkeeping and each is new this round. First, the flagship deployment run is no longer evidenced as live: `exp11-fullscale.json` records `materializationMs: 0`, `ledgerSpendDeltaUsd: 0`, `wallMs: 3131`, and `certifyWallMs` of 768 ms and 861 ms for 135 and 405 supposedly live oracle calls, while the prose says "certified the formatting edit live, with the real model as oracle" and attaches \$10.49, 7.2 hours, and 7 cell errors to it. Reviewers A, C, and D each traced the live ledger and found the run that wrote this artifact billed zero cells and zero dollars across eight consecutive rows at 03:19:18-19, exactly the eight `runColumn` invocations the two sweeps make. The r4 panel could verify 25,801,689 ms and \$9.38559188 from this file. An r5 referee can verify neither, because the run that produced them was overwritten by one that spent nothing. The hours and the error count are hardcoded constants in the generator, and the error count is contradicted by the artifact's own `covered = 89,184 = n`.

Second, the macro pass numerically inverted the paper's central narrative. `\floorBoolFresh = 7.22%` is not a self-flip rate. `exp10-multidraw.ts:117-131` binds `d1` to v1's prompt and `d2` to v2's prompt, so the diagonal rate the macro reports is the formatting edit's **cross-version** flip rate. The meta-reviewer recomputed from `exp10-labels.json`: cross-version pairs 143/1998 = 7.157%, 142/2000 = 7.100%, 148/1999 = 7.404%, mean 7.2203%; the genuine within-version self-flip rate, the quantity the paper defines `s` to be, is 302/5,996 = 5.037% (v1), 296/5,998 = 4.935% (v2), 598/11,994 = 4.986% pooled. Substituting the edit rate for the floor makes the Table 1 caption assert that a 6.0% flip rate "sits barely above" a 7.22% floor it is below, makes `main.tex:418` print the same inversion, and sets the impossibility floor at `s = 7.22%` where `table1.tex:8-9` certifies the same stratum at alpha = 0.05 in 19% of replications and at alpha = 0.10 in 99%. The hand-typed "about 5%" at `main.tex:101` is now the only correct floor in the paper and it is contradicted by the macro inside its own parenthesis.

Third, the same-class contradiction the panel has now flagged in five consecutive rounds reappeared in the paragraph rewritten to fix it. "The oracle budget was *identical* at both scales, `\benchOracle` calls at alpha=0.2 and `\benchOracleTight` at alpha=0.1" renders as "identical at both scales, 135 calls at alpha=0.2 and 765 at alpha=0.1", and the paper prints the refutation three lines above: "At alpha=0.1 the schedule escalated to 405 calls". Identity holds only at alpha=0.2. Worse, the r4-era exp11 spent 765 at alpha=0.1 on the identical seeded configuration and this run spent 405, which is the paper's own proof that the oracle count is a high-variance random variable and simultaneously discharges the r4 finding (R6) that the bolded 135 is a single favorable draw.

Beyond those, the released "independent reproducible audit" added to close r4's R5 contradicts the paper by a factor of thirty (`exp11b-verify.json`: 60 rows, 0 flips, 0.00% against the paper's 3,011 rows at 2.76%), is cited nowhere in `main.tex`, and the panel splits three ways on why. The remaining statistical holes are the ones flagged three rounds running: Theorem 1's proof sketch still asserts the without-replacement domination Assumption 3 explicitly declines to prove, defended in `sivm.ts:16-18` by "small sampling fractions" that reach 80.9% in the pinned runs; B2 is called "identical adaptive machinery" while running `maxLooks = 5` against the pinned 6, which plausibly manufactures the stratification-hierarchy result; the savings ceiling of 91.35% (one minus the cached-TRUE share) is unstated while Limitations claims growth without bound, and the run has already reached 91.25% of it; and there is still no Wilson interval, percentile, median, McNemar, or TOST anywhere in the paper. One non-numeric objection is now the second-strongest reason to reject and no amount of regeneration touches it: there is still no head-to-head against SUPG, the "reduction" is a single parenthetical, none of the three baselines is a competing system, and Stale View Cleaning has been absent for four rounds of being asked for.

Almost every fix is zero-API. The exceptions are one honest re-run of exp11 and one SUPG baseline. But the count of live text-versus-artifact contradictions went from 7 to 22, not down, and the panel's standing condition from round 2 applies with full force: this closes only if it closes with zero new hand-typed numbers, and this round proved that moving them into the generator does not count.

## Panel disagreements, resolved

Five points where reviewers diverged, adjudicated against the tree as it stands.

1. **Does `exp11b-verify.ts` reproduce?** Three-way split. B and E report re-running it (E "unmodified against the current repo and DB") and obtaining 1,811 audited rows with 50 flips = 2.76% at alpha=0.2 and 1,808/50 = 2.77% at alpha=0.1, matching exp11's `auditComposition` exactly. D traced it to the seeded `ORDER BY RAND` sample not reproducing across processes, noting that only 67 of exp11's 3,011 persisted `auditedRowIds` fall in a `SELECT response_id` variant of the sample and that 67 minus the sampled prefix is exp11b's 60. **Adjudication:** the meta-reviewer verified that both scripts issue the byte-identical query string `SELECT * FROM profiles ORDER BY RAND(?) LIMIT ?` with `(42, 2000)` (`exp11-fullscale.ts:108`, `exp11b-verify.ts:42-45`), so a projection-list difference cannot be the proximate cause **as stated in the paper's own caveat**. The "reconstruction ordering" theory that A and C advance is arithmetically insufficient: exp11b removes `slice(st.sampled)`, a 90-row prefix, from an 81,469-member stratum, so any shuffle-order mismatch can move the audited count by at most 90, never from 1,811 to 60. The only quantity that can cut 1,811 to 60 is a different `labelSet` or a different `v2Oracle` map, so the seeded sample was not reproduced by the run that wrote the committed artifact, whatever the proximate cause.
