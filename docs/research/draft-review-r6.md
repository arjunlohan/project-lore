## Consensus verdict

The round-5 close-out path's items (1) and (2) were executed and they worked. The floor inversion that inverted the paper's central narrative is genuinely, independently, arithmetically fixed. Four reviewers and the meta-reviewer each recomputed both quantities from the raw 2,000-row label dump rather than accepting the changelog, and all five got the same numbers to four decimal places: within-version self-flip is 598/11,994 = 4.9858% (`\floorBool` = 5.0%), cross-version paired draws are 433/5,997 = 7.2203% (`\editFlipFreshBoth` = 7.2%), with per-diagonal counts 143/1998, 142/2000, 148/1999. `gen-paper-assets.ts:164-188` now loads `exp10-labels.json` and derives both instead of typing either. Table 1's caption, the Section 2 floor, and the stability-law paragraph now order correctly, and the Setup paragraph labels the fresh-both instrument explicitly. The correction also silently repaired F11's *empirical* refutation: at s = 4.99% the alpha = 0.05 and alpha = 0.10 certifications no longer contradict the floor.

Five further r5 items closed and were verified by recomputation, not assertion. The oracle-call distribution is real and reported (`oracleMean` 201.51 to 202, `oracleP50` 225, range 135-270, with the seeded 135 correctly identified as that distribution's *minimum* and as "a favourable draw"). The false "identical at both scales" sentence is gone and replaced by the measured 765-versus-405 result. `exp11b-verify.ts` now audits the run's persisted `auditedRowIds` and agrees with `exp11`'s `auditComposition` to sixteen significant figures (1,811 rows, 50 flips, 0.027609055770292656). `exp7` was re-run at the pinned six looks and the result got *stronger* and was reported that way. F15's guard throws without `EXP_STRATIFIER`. The generated layer is now beyond dispute: reviewer D re-ran the generator against the live repository and MySQL and obtained `table1.tex`, `figdata.tex` and `macros.tex` byte-identically, and traced 105 of 109 macros to a source artifact or the live ledger with all 105 correct.

Then the revision spent its new material. Every reject-level finding this round is in code written to close r5.

**The SUPG head-to-head is not a matched comparison, on five independent axes, and all five reviewers found at least two of them.** `\hhSivmErr` (2.32%) is sIVM's presented-cells rate over the 1,814-row certified stratum; `\hhSupgErr` (6.22%) is SUPG's rate among its 1,769 reused cells. Section 2 of this paper exists to explain that those differ and that the reuse-set rate is strictly larger, and the flagship baseline paragraph then prints one of each. Like-for-like the pair is 2.57% against 6.22%, or 42 wrong cells against 110 out of 2,000 presented. `exp13-supg.ts` gives sIVM `DELTA / strata.length` (0.05, then 0.05/6 = 0.00833 per look) and gives the SUPG arm the full `DELTA` = 0.1, evaluated at up to ~215 candidate thresholds with the maximum-selecting one kept and no union bound: a 12x looser level plus an uncorrected argmax, which is roughly the size of the 8% volume gap the paper reports as SUPG's win. The threshold never binds, because both alphas sit far above the 6.0% population flip rate: SUPG selects 1,769 of 1,775 unlabelled rows (99.66%) and 1,234 of 1,235 (99.92%), so "SUPG-style" as run *is* B3 with a rubber stamp. The proxy handed to it is the embedding-interaction score this paper measures at AUC 0.408, below chance on the very edit, while sIVM gets the informative cached value; the cached-value binary proxy that Section 3 says *is* the reduction, and that `exp13`'s own docstring names, is never run. And the paragraph prints sIVM numbers (225 oracle, 1,634 reused) that contradict Table 1's (135, 1,724) for the identical pair, alpha and procedure, with no reconciliation.

**The deployment materialization cost is the wrong ledger slice, and the error is at the source-code level.** `gen-paper-assets.ts:349-358` issues `SELECT SUM(cost_usd), SUM(cells) FROM cost_ledger JOIN ai_columns WHERE col.name LIKE '%(lab)%'` with no run, time, or kind filter, so `\deployMatCost` = \$10.10 over `\deployMatCells` = 85,862 is the column's **lifetime** spend. Reviewers B and C independently decomposed it row by row and agree to the cent: ledger id 26 (78,956 cells, \$9.30625542) is the corpus materialization; ids 7-9 from the previous day (5,819 cells, \$0.66806334) are the n = 2,000 benchmark label passes; ids 27-44 (1,087 cells, \$0.12326132) are the certification passes' own oracle calls. The three sum to 85,862 cells and \$10.09758008 exactly. So the headline economic comparison overstates its own recompute baseline by 8.6% in the flattering direction, *and* counts the maintenance oracle calls inside the baseline they are being compared against. Ledger-sourced is not the same as correctly attributed, exactly as relocating literals into a generated file was not the same as deriving them.

**The sentence added to close F12 is mathematically false.** Assumption 3 and the proof sketch now both assert that finite-population bounds are "valid by construction," "strictly sharper," and "the correct replacement" / "a drop-in replacement." Both halves cannot hold at once: a valid without-replacement bound that dominated Maurer-Pontil pointwise would prove Theorem 1 by sandwiching and close the gap Assumption 3 says is open. The numerics agree with the logic. The meta-reviewer confirmed A's arithmetic: at the flagship certificate (n = 90, k = 1, per-look delta = 0.05/6) the Bardenet-Maillard empirical-Bernstein-Serfling *linear term alone*, kappa·log(5/delta)/n with kappa = 7/3 + 3/sqrt(2) = 4.45465, is 0.31662, already exceeding alpha = 0.2 regardless of the variance term, against MP's 0.19158 (which reproduces from the artifact to 16 digits). At alpha = 0.1 (n = 360, k = 9) BM lands near 0.134 against MP's 0.08790. Adopting the "correct replacement" would refuse both flagship certificates and every headline number in the abstract.

Beyond those three, the pattern is sharp and worth naming: **everything routed through the generator is now correct and reproducible; everything left as prose has stayed wrong.** The availability statement's "every quantitative claim ... cited through macros rather than typed inline" is still false, with roughly eighteen live hand-typed measurement numerals, three of which contradict their own macros (prose 0.467 against `\aucVcacheF` 0.468, prose 0.407 against `\aucInterF` 0.408, prose "0.94--0.99" against `\simLo`/`\simHi` 0.96/0.99), and the orphan count rose from 12 to 16 rather than falling. The two enforcement mechanisms r5 specified, a no-string-literal assertion in the generator and a CI grep over `main.tex`, were not built, so this recurs a fifth time without them. N7 survives verbatim (82.9% bootstrap mean against 91.2% single realization, with the like-for-like 86.2% emitted nowhere). The alpha = 0.1 oracle distribution is computed and thrown away, and the 765 the paper does print is that distribution's *maximum* while the deployment's 405 is its *median*, which is the strongest possible statement of the paper's own variance argument, generated and discarded. The restored oracle column now refutes F8's "4x cheaper" futility claim on the same page. The F10r re-run invalidated a stratification-hierarchy sentence nobody updated. Stale View Cleaning has been absent for five rounds with the entry and the positioning delta already written in this repository's own audit file. And the paper still contains zero intervals of any kind: `grep` returns nothing for Wilson, percentile, McNemar, TOST, "confidence interval," or even the bare word "interval," and exactly one "median."

The panel's summary judgment, which every reviewer reached independently and which is why the vote moved: the statistical spine is sound for the first time, the remaining list is longer in count but materially shorter in effort than r5's, and essentially all of it is zero-API. The two items that needed money, the second model and the SUPG run, both landed. The one remaining API cost the panel would spend is roughly \$0.20 to extend `exp12` above its own floor.

### M3. [reject-level · MANDATORY · 1/5 with the numerics, 2/5 on the logical tension] The Bardenet-Maillard sentence added to close F12 is mathematically false and self-defeating

`paper/main.tex:217-220` (Assumption 3) and `:306-311` (proof sketch); `packages/core/src/sivm.ts:16-18`.

The paper now asserts twice that finite-population bounds are "valid by construction, sharper, and a drop-in replacement" and "valid by construction here and strictly sharper, and are the correct replacement." Both halves cannot be true at once. **The logic:** if a valid without-replacement bound U_wor were pointwise <= the Maurer-Pontil bound U_MP, then {U_MP <= alpha} is a subset of {U_wor <= alpha}, so P(certify with MP and p > alpha) <= P(U_wor < p) <= delta, and Theorem 1 would be *proved*, contradicting the gap Assumption 3 says is open one hundred lines earlier. **The numerics** (independently reproduced by the meta-reviewer): at the flagship deployment certificate (n = 90, k = 1, N = 81,469, per-look delta = 0.05/6) the MP bound is 0.19158474122511077, matching the artifact digit for digit, while the Bardenet-Maillard empirical-Bernstein-Serfling bound is roughly 0.367; its linear term alone, kappa·log(5/delta)/n with kappa = 7/3 + 3/sqrt(2) = 4.454654, is **0.31662**, which already exceeds alpha = 0.2 regardless of the variance term. At the alpha = 0.1 deployment certificate (n = 360, k = 9) BM lands near 0.134 against MP's 0.08790. BM is sharper only in the deep-sampling regime (Djinni formatting, n = 1,617 of 2,000: 0.1535 against 0.1580), which is exactly the configuration producing the paper's *worst* savings (21.0%). Adopting the "correct replacement" would refuse both flagship certificates and every headline number in the abstract. *Required fix:* delete "strictly sharper" and "drop-in replacement" from both sites and state the truth: the BM empirical-Bernstein-Serfling bound is valid without replacement but is **looser** than Maurer-Pontil at small m, because it carries log(5/delta) and a kappa ~ 4.45 linear term; at the paper's certifying look depths it would refuse, and it becomes sharper only above roughly 70% sampling fraction. ### M7. [major · MANDATORY · 4/5] The second-model experiment tests only the trivial direction, and the abstract states the result as a universal

### M8. [major · MANDATORY · 3/5] The second model's floor and edit rate use different instruments from the primary model's, and its decode pinning is unverified
